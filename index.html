Smart Money Concepts strategy

How to read a 35% win rate. It means about 1 trade in 3 hits its target. That alone doesn’t say whether a strategy makes or loses money: what matters is how big the winners are compared with the losers, and how risk is managed. The bot targets a 1:3 reward-to-risk, and at that ratio a strategy needs roughly 25% of its trades to win just to break even (before spreads and fees). But that only holds if winners actually reach target, so we keep logging every trade, win or loss, to check it. Ricknova Bot is still being improved and tested. Treat it as a tool, not a promise.
